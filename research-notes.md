### Service Mesh definition
The service mesh architecture is an application infrastructure layer on top of cloud-native applications. Service mesh has gained popularity since 2017, and it is still a relatively young concept. A service mesh provides a layer of abstraction above your applications. <br>

The service mesh could secure communication between the microservices with TLS. The benefit here is that each developer no longer has to implement TLS encryption and decryption that's specific to the language they are writing in.<br>
